Make the work reviewable

A concise portfolio-entry checklist

  • State the authorization, scope, objective, and stop condition.
  • Explain the method without publishing secrets or sensitive details.
  • Link each factual conclusion to redacted evidence.
  • Record the decision, result, limitations, cleanup, and next step.
