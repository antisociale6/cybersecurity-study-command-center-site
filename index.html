Inside Month 10

Engineer and Tune a Detection

The outcome

Turn a documented threat hypothesis into a testable local detection with known telemetry requirements, reproducible benign test evidence, false-positive analysis, and a practical analyst handoff.

Time box: 7 to 9 hours across four focused sessions. Work in short sessions and stop when the stated evidence exists. The objective is a defensible result, not tool volume.

Finish line

What you produce if you complete the sprint

  • A behavior-focused detection hypothesis with MITRE ATT&CK mapping and telemetry requirements
  • A bounded baseline, data-quality assessment, and evidence-backed exclusion proposal
  • A readable detection query or structured pseudocode with positive and negative tests
  • A tuning diary and before-and-after regression metrics
  • An analyst triage runbook with escalation criteria, closure reasons, and residual blind spots

Safe scenario

A fictional Northstar workstation records PowerShell and process activity, but the team has no written explanation of what its alerts are intended to detect or how an analyst should validate them. You will design one behavior-focused detection using supplied synthetic events and a harmless local file-creation signal, map it to MITRE ATT&CK, and tune it without running malware, obfuscation, credential-access tools, or an adversary-emulation framework.
