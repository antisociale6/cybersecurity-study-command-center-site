# Synthetic Incident Response Tabletop and After-Action Review - Free Cybersecurity Portfolio Template

Use this blank worksheet to turn one owned, synthetic, or explicitly authorized
lab into reviewable evidence. Replace every prompt with your own facts. Delete
instructions that do not apply instead of inventing an answer.

## Completion check

- [ ] The owner, authorization, scope, and stop condition are visible.
- [ ] Every conclusion points to evidence or is labeled as an assumption.
- [ ] No credentials, secrets, personal data, customer details, usernames,
      hostnames, exact local paths, private URLs, or private authorization
      records are included.
- [ ] Synthetic values are unmistakably fake and sensitive evidence is redacted.
- [ ] Cleanup, rollback, remaining limitations, and the next review are recorded.
- [ ] The final write-up says what the exercise does **not** prove.
