Completion check

  • The owner, authorization, scope, and stop condition are visible.
  • Every conclusion points to evidence or is labeled as an assumption.
  • No credentials, secrets, personal data, customer details, usernames, hostnames, exact local paths, private URLs, or private authorization records are included.
  • Synthetic values are unmistakably fake and sensitive evidence is redacted.
  • Cleanup, rollback, remaining limitations, and the next review are recorded.
  • The final write-up says what the exercise does not prove.
