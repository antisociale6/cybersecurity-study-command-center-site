Validate Segmentation and Firewall Policy

An evidence-led method for translating approved communication needs into least-access network policy and reviewable validation cases.

Useful when searching for: network segmentation validation checklist

A practical, evidence-led method

  1. Define an isolated lab or fictional network with named owners, synthetic services, approved communication needs, and a restore plan.
  2. Document each required flow by source, destination, protocol category, purpose, data sensitivity, direction, and review date.
  3. Translate the approved flows into a least-access policy design that denies undocumented communication by default.
  4. Create expected allow and deny cases and identify the local evidence that would distinguish correct behavior from uncertainty.
  5. Record results, exceptions, rollback decisions, residual limitations, and an owner for periodic policy review.

Safety boundary

Use only an isolated, owned, or explicitly authorized lab with synthetic services. Do not scan public ranges, test shared networks, alter organizational firewalls, or capture unrelated traffic.

What to prove

Aim to produce a reviewable artifact such as Least-Access Network Segmentation Validation. Keep facts, assumptions, limits, and redactions visible so a reviewer can understand what the work demonstrates and what it does not.
