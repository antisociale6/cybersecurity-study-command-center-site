SBOM Review Workflow for Students

For an owned sample project, normalize component names, versions, suppliers, relationships, and missing fields; verify a sample against project evidence; then assess applicable official advisories and document an owned, time-bound risk decision.

Curriculum activity: Produce and Use an SBOM

This note helps you create: SBOM Quality Review and Dependency Risk Decision

A practical, evidence-led method

  1. Choose an owned sample project and record its purpose, source boundary, build context, and expected dependency sources.
  2. Create or obtain an inventory of components and normalize names, versions, suppliers, relationships, and missing fields.
  3. Check inventory quality by tracing a sample of entries back to project evidence and documenting uncertainty.
  4. Compare relevant dependency signals with current official advisories and distinguish exposure from mere component presence.
  5. Record a risk decision, accountable owner, proposed response, validation evidence, and a date for reassessment.

Safety boundary

Use only an owned or explicitly authorized sample project. Do not upload proprietary manifests, source code, internal package names, credentials, or customer information to public services.

Copy-ready artifact outline

Start a document named SBOM Quality Review and Dependency Risk Decision and use these five sections. Replace every prompt with evidence from your owned, synthetic, or explicitly authorized lab.

  1. Project and build context

    Record the owned sample project's purpose, source boundary, build method, review date, expected package sources, and information excluded from public tools.

  2. Component inventory

    Capture normalized component name, version, supplier, source, relationship, license field, and any missing identity information without exposing proprietary names.

  3. Inventory-quality sample

    Trace a representative set of entries back to project evidence and record matches, mismatches, unknowns, duplicate identities, and confidence.

  4. Advisory and exposure decision

    For each relevant official advisory, separate component presence from actual exposure and record affected conditions, evidence, and remaining uncertainty.

  5. Risk response and reassessment

    Document the decision, accountable owner, proposed response, compatibility concern, validation evidence, and date when the evidence must be reviewed again.

Before you call it complete

  • Authorization and scope are visible.
  • Every conclusion points to evidence or is labeled as an assumption.
  • Sensitive details are removed or redacted.
  • Cleanup, rollback, and remaining limitations are recorded.

What to prove

Aim to produce a reviewable artifact such as SBOM Quality Review and Dependency Risk Decision. Keep facts, assumptions, limits, and redactions visible so a reviewer can understand what the work demonstrates and what it does not.

Primary references

Check your decisions against these official sources. Record the version or access date you used because guidance can change.

  • Minimum Elements for a Software Bill of Materials - CISA

    Use this government baseline to decide which component, dependency, provenance, generation, coverage, and update fields an SBOM should contain.

  • CycloneDX Specification Overview

    Use the current specification overview to normalize component identities, relationships, metadata, and machine-readable SBOM formats.
