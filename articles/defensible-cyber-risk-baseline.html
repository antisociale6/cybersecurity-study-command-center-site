Build a Defensible Cyber-Risk Baseline

An evidence-led method for documenting assets, risks, current safeguards, and priority decisions in a controlled cybersecurity study scenario.

Useful when searching for: cyber risk baseline template for students

A practical, evidence-led method

  1. Define a fictional or explicitly authorized organization, its decision owner, and the narrow scope of the review.
  2. List the important services, information, dependencies, and responsibilities using only facts that can be evidenced.
  3. Describe plausible adverse events and connect each one to an affected asset and observable impact.
  4. Record current safeguards, evidence gaps, assumptions, and limitations without treating missing evidence as protection.
  5. Prioritize a small set of actions by risk, effort, reversibility, owner, and the evidence that would show progress.

Safety boundary

Use only fictional, synthetic, owned, or explicitly authorized information. Do not inspect live third-party systems, collect personal data, or present the review as certification.

What to prove

Aim to produce a reviewable artifact such as Cyber-Risk Baseline for a Synthetic Small Business. Keep facts, assumptions, limits, and redactions visible so a reviewer can understand what the work demonstrates and what it does not.
